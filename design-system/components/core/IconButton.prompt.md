Compact icon-only button for toolbars and row affordances. Always pass `aria-label`.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="more-horizontal" aria-label="More options" />
<IconButton icon="plus" variant="solid" aria-label="Add entry" />
<IconButton icon="pencil" variant="outline" size="sm" aria-label="Edit" />
```

Variants: `ghost | solid | outline`. Sizes: `sm | md | lg`.
